    Synthesis

    The Bottom Line

    Team and users agree on the core truth: trust, reliability, and respect for people's time are non-negotiable. The team's 3–3 split between EarnWise and Savi resolves when filtered through user data — users overwhelmingly want EarnWise's clarity with Savi's warmth. "Fun to use" scored 0% as a user priority. The next iteration should feel like a trusted financial tool, not a toy.

    Convergence

    Where Team & Users Align

    Four areas where both streams point the same direction. These are settled.

    Settled
    Trust is the foundation
    Team: 5 of 6 rated trust "just right" on at least one direction.
    Users: Reliable payouts is the #1 priority (37%). Proof of payment is the #1 acquisition trigger (35%). Scam concerns are a top-4 churn driver (43%).
    Settled
    Professional, not playful
    Team: Professionalism rated "just right" across both directions by nearly everyone.
    Users: "Fun to use" scored 0% as a priority. 65% want reliability + value. 9% want simplicity.
    Settled
    Real dollars, not points
    Team: Valentina loved "seeing the real balance in money equivalent."
    Users: 51% prefer dollar amounts. Only 11% want points. Open-ended: "tasks that earn dollars instead of points or coins."
    Settled
    Goals over expense anchoring
    Team: Matt T: "References to gas and groceries add to my stress."
    Users: 55% spend on expenses, 41% on treats — both framings exist, but goal-based avoids the stress trigger.

    Tensions

    Where They Diverge

    Four areas where the team splits or conflicts with user data. Each needs a clear resolution.

    Playfulness calibration

    Team says
    Split. EarnWise: "too serious" (Aditya, Matt R), "too sleepy" (Matt T, Jamil). Savi: "too cute" (Matt R), "too cozy" (Matt T, Aditya).
    Users say
    Fun = 0% priority. Pragmatic earners who want dependable value. Not a single respondent chose "fun" as what matters most.
    Resolution
    Warm professionalism. Think trusted credit union, not children's game. Personality through copy tone and micro-interactions, not visual playfulness.

    The mascot question

    Team says
    Mixed enthusiasm. Valentina, Corinna, Matt T all open to a mascot. But: fox overlaps competitors (Scrambly). No consensus on species.
    Users say
    Nobody asked for one. Zero mentions in 46 open-ended responses. Their priorities: payouts, speed, trust.
    Resolution
    Nice-to-have, not must-have. Test a non-fox mascot variant vs. no mascot in next round. If kept, must feel mature and functional (guide, not decoration).

    Warmth level

    Team says
    Contradictory. EarnWise: "too cozy" (Matt T) vs. "too corporate" (Jamil, Matt R). Savi: "too cold" (Valentina) vs. "too cozy" (Matt T, Aditya, Matt R).
    Users say
    They want reliability (37%) and value (28%). The warmth they seek is emotional safety — knowing the app won't scam them.
    Resolution
    Warmth from behavior, not aesthetics. Transparent earnings, fast payouts, clear communication. Visual: clean and confident with subtle organic touches.

    Gamification energy

    Team says
    Aditya: "EarnWise lacks the excitement gamification requires." Savi's fun element is needed for game mechanics.
    Users say
    Love activities (87% videos, 79% games, 77% check-ins) but don't care if they're "fun" — they care if they pay.
    Resolution
    Rewarding, not entertaining. Progress bars toward goals, streaks with forgiveness, clear cause-and-effect. Brief, dignified celebrations — not confetti.

    Direction

    Five Decisions for the Next Iteration

    Based on combined evidence from both streams. Each has a clear rationale.

    1
    Brand Tone: Trustworthy Clarity
    EarnWise's visual clarity + Savi's conversational warmth, calibrated toward professionalism. The tone should feel like a friend who's good with money — straightforward, reliable, occasionally warm, never condescending.
    Evidence: Team professionalism ratings + user 0% fun + 37% reliability priority
    2
    Mascot: Test or Drop
    Create two screen variants — one with a non-fox mascot (owl? hedgehog?) in a functional guide role, one without. Test in next round. If it survives, it appears in onboarding and empty states only.
    Evidence: Team interest but no species consensus + user silence + competitor fox overlap
    3
    Earnings Framing: Goals First
    Primary: User-set goals with custom labels ("coffee fund," "vacation"). Secondary: Cumulative lifetime total. Optional: Expense context as a user-controlled toggle — never system-imposed.
    Evidence: Matt T stress feedback + strategy doc revision + user 55/41 spending split
    4
    Onboarding: Simple + Proof
    From EarnWise: One-page demographics, fewer clicks. From Savi: Time-awareness questions. From users: Show proof of real payouts early. First-task-to-first-dollar in under 5 minutes. $1.00 first cashout minimum.
    Evidence: Valentina on simplicity + Corinna on personalization + 35% need proof to try
    5
    Visual: EarnWise Palette, Savi Touches
    Base: EarnWise palette and typography (stands out from competitors). Add: Savi's personalization (name, photo, custom goals) + subtle organic corners and transitions. Remove: Movie-moment illustrations, heavy mascot, cozy color washes.
    Evidence: Valentina's palette praise + Corinna's Savi UX praise + user pragmatism data
