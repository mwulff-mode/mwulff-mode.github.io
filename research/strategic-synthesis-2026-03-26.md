# Strategic Synthesis: Team Feedback + User Survey

**Date:** March 26, 2026
**Purpose:** Combine internal team brand direction feedback (6 respondents) with external user survey data (43-58 respondents) into one strategic guide for the next design iteration.

---

## Executive Summary

Team and users converge on the same core truth: **trust, reliability, and respect for people's time are non-negotiable.** The team's 3-3 split between EarnWise and Savi resolves when filtered through user data -- users overwhelmingly want EarnWise's clarity and professionalism, with selective warmth from Savi's personalization. "Fun to use" scored literally 0% as a user priority, which recalibrates the playfulness dial downward. The next iteration should combine EarnWise's visual identity and dollar-forward transparency with Savi's conversational onboarding and personal touch -- delivered in a tone that feels like a trusted financial tool, not a toy.

## 1. Where Team & Users Align

These are settled decisions -- both streams point the same direction.

### Trust is the foundation

| Signal | Source | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Trust rating "just right" | Team (both directions) | 5 of 6 rated trust "just right" on at least one direction |
| #1 priority: reliable payouts | Users (Q6) | 37% -- top-ranked by a wide margin |
| #1 acquisition trigger: proof it pays | Users (Q13) | 35% -- social proof drives trial |
| #4 churn driver: feels scammy | Users (Q7) | 43% would leave over trust concerns |

**Decision:** Trust-building is not a feature -- it's the entire brand strategy. Every design decision should pass the test: "Does this make us feel more trustworthy?"

### Professionalism over playfulness

| Signal | Source | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Professionalism "just right" | Team | Rated "just right" across both directions by nearly all respondents |
| "Fun to use" as a priority | Users (Q6) | 0% -- zero votes out of 54 |
| What they actually want | Users (Q6) | 65% want reliability + value; 9% want simplicity |

**Decision:** The app should feel like a reliable financial tool that respects your intelligence. Warmth: yes. Cuteness: no.

### Real dollars, not points

| Signal | Source | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Valentina's feedback | Team | "I love the opportunity to see the real balance you have earned in money equivalent" |
| Earnings display preference | Users (Q8) | 51% prefer dollar amounts; only 11% want points |
| Open-ended frustration | Users (Q13) | "More profitable tasks that can earn dollars instead of points or coins" |

**Decision:** Show actual money. Always. No coins, no tokens, no conversion math.

### Goal-based framing over expense anchoring

| Signal | Source | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Matt T's feedback | Team | "I don't like the references to gas and groceries. It adds to my stress." |
| Spending patterns | Users (Q3) | 55% everyday expenses, 41% treats -- both motivations are real |
| Earnings framing strategy | Existing doc | Goal-based framing already promoted to primary system (March 23 revision) |

**Decision:** Goal-based framing is the primary system. Expense context is optional and user-controlled -- never system-imposed.

## 2. Where They Diverge (& How to Resolve)

These are areas where team opinion splits or conflicts with user data. Each needs a clear resolution.

### Playfulness calibration

**Team says:** Split. EarnWise critiqued as "too serious/dry" (Aditya, Matt R) and "too calm/sleepy" (Matt T, Jamil). Savi critiqued as "too cute/childish" (Matt R) and "too cozy/soft" (Matt T, Aditya).

**Users say:** Fun = 0% priority. Pragmatic earners who want dependable value.

**Resolution:** The sweet spot is **warm professionalism** -- think a trusted credit union, not a children's game. Inject personality through copy tone and micro-interactions, not through visual playfulness. Savi's conversational warmth works; Savi's visual cuteness doesn't.

### The mascot question

**Team says:** Mixed enthusiasm.
- Valentina: "Maybe a mascot -- a mouse, pig, squirrel -- to make an association"
- Corinna: "The fox is very cute and probably will go well with both men and women"
- Matt T: "I like the idea of a mascot (not sure about a fox)"
- Valentina (also): "Fox is popular among competitors" (Scrambly uses one)

**Users say:** Nobody asked for a mascot. Zero mentions in 46 open-ended responses. Their priorities are payouts, speed, and trust.

**Resolution:** Mascot is a **nice-to-have, not a must-have.** If we pursue one, it must:
1. Not be a fox (competitor overlap with Scrambly)
2. Feel trustworthy and mature, not childish (owl? hedgehog?)
3. Serve a functional purpose (guide, not decoration)
4. Be testable -- include a mascot variant and a no-mascot variant in next round

### Warmth level

**Team says:** Contradictory feedback across respondents.
- EarnWise: "too cozy/soft" (Matt T) vs. "too corporate" (Jamil, Matt R)
- Savi: "too cold/clinical" (Valentina) vs. "too cozy/soft" (Matt T, Aditya, Matt R)

**Users say:** They want reliability (37%) and value (28%). The warmth they seek is **emotional safety** -- knowing the app won't scam them -- not visual warmth.

**Resolution:** Warmth should come from **behavior, not aesthetics:**
- Warmth = transparent earnings, fast payouts, clear communication
- Visual warmth = clean, calm, confident (EarnWise palette) with subtle organic touches (rounded corners, gentle transitions)
- NOT warmth = soft colors, cozy illustrations, cute mascot energy

### Gamification energy

**Team says:** Aditya raised a key concern: "EarnWise lacks the excitement that gamification requires, a bit extravaganza. The pro with Savi is the fun element which gamification requires."

**Users say:** They love the activities (87% videos, 79% games, 77% check-ins) but don't care if they're "fun" -- they care if they pay.

**Resolution:** Gamification should feel **rewarding, not entertaining.** Think:
- Progress bars that fill toward a goal (not flashy animations)
- Streaks with forgiveness (not punishing FOMO)
- Clear cause-and-effect: "You did X, you earned $Y"
- Celebration moments that are brief and dignified, not confetti explosions

## 3. Five Strategic Decisions for Next Iteration

### 1. Brand Tone: Trustworthy Clarity

**Formula:** EarnWise's visual clarity + Savi's conversational warmth, calibrated toward professionalism.

The tone should feel like a friend who's good with money -- straightforward, reliable, occasionally warm, never condescending. Copy should be first-person and direct. Avoid financial jargon. Avoid cutesy language.

**Evidence:** Team professionalism ratings + user 0% fun priority + user 37% reliability priority.

### 2. Mascot: Test or Drop

**Next step:** Create two variants of key screens -- one with a subtle non-fox mascot (functional guide role), one without. Test in next round.

If mascot survives testing, it should:
- Be a species with trust/wisdom associations (owl, hedgehog, squirrel -- NOT fox)
- Appear in onboarding and empty states, not everywhere
- Have a name but not a full personality system

**Evidence:** Team interest but no consensus on species + user silence on mascot desire + competitor overlap risk.

### 3. Earnings Framing: Goals First

**Primary system:** User-set goals with labels they choose ("coffee fund," "vacation savings," "rainy day").

**Secondary:** Cumulative lifetime total prominently displayed. Weekly progress summaries.

**Optional:** Expense context as a toggle -- users who want to see "that's 2 gallons of gas" can opt in. Never default.

**Evidence:** Matt T stress feedback + existing strategy doc revision + user spending split (55% expenses / 41% treats).

### 4. Onboarding: Simple + Proof

Combine the best of both directions:
- **From EarnWise:** All demographic questions on one page, fewer clicks (Valentina's feedback)
- **From Savi:** Time-awareness questions -- "When do you have time to play?" (Corinna loved this)
- **From users:** Show proof of real payouts early in onboarding (35% need "proof it pays out" to try)
- **New:** First-task-to-first-dollar should be under 5 minutes. $1.00 minimum first cashout.

**Evidence:** Valentina on simplicity + Corinna on personalization + user acquisition data.

### 5. Visual Direction: EarnWise Palette, Savi Touches

**Base:** EarnWise color palette and typography (Valentina: "colour scheme stands out from competitors")

**Layer in:**
- Savi's personalization moments (user name, photo, custom goal labels)
- Subtle organic touches (slightly rounded corners, gentle easing on transitions)
- Savi's approach to showing time-relevant content

**Remove:**
- Savi's movie-moment illustration style (Corinna: "really looks different than the rest")
- Heavy mascot presence
- Overly cozy/soft color washes

**Evidence:** Team preference split resolves toward EarnWise visual + Savi UX + user pragmatism data.

## 6. Open Questions for Next Round

These need resolution through testing or team discussion:

1. **Mascot: yes or no?** Test both variants. If yes, which species?
2. **Exact color palette:** EarnWise base confirmed, but does it need any warmth injection from Savi?
3. **Gamification depth:** How much gamification energy can the EarnWise visual system support? (Aditya's concern)
4. **Onboarding proof moment:** What specific "proof" do we show? Real user testimonials? Live payout feed? Earnings calculator?
5. **Expense anchoring toggle:** Is the optional expense context worth building, or should we cut scope and go goals-only?
