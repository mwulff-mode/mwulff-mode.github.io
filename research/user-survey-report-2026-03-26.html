    So What

    Strategic Implications

    What this means for product, brand, and go-to-market.

    Product Must-Haves

    1. PayPal payouts at launch — 80% use it, 69% prefer it for cashout
    2. Fast processing — Target instant or same-day; 73% expect <24 hours
    3. Low cashout minimums — High minimums are a top-3 churn driver (45%)
    4. Core loop: videos + games + daily check-ins — All score 77%+ interest
    5. Show earnings in real dollars — Not points, not coins, not tokens

    Brand calibration: "Fun to use" scored 0%. Playfulness should be a seasoning, not a main ingredient. Tone should feel reliable, straightforward, and respectful. This audience responds to proof and transparency, not personality and charm.

    Acquisition Strategy

    1. Cross-promotion from existing apps — #1 channel at 40%
    2. Social proof everywhere — Payout screenshots, testimonials, 4.3+ app store rating
    3. Facebook as primary paid social — 61% of users are on it daily
    4. Sign-up bonus — Works for 20% of potential users as a hook

    Earnings Framing

    • Target $25–$50/month — the sweet spot for 40% of users
    • Goal-based framing may outperform expense anchoring per team feedback
    • Avoid anything that feels like "pennies" — it's the #1 churn trigger
