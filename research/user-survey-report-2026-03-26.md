# New Rewarded App — User Survey Report

**Date:** March 26, 2026
**Platform:** Maze (unmoderated)
**Sample:** 43–58 respondents (varies by question)
**Audience:** Existing Mode Earn users, primarily US-based, 45+ demographic
**Device:** Predominantly Android / Chrome mobile

---

## Executive Summary

This survey validates our core assumptions about the target audience and surfaces clear product priorities. The respondents are pragmatic earners (45+, mostly retired or semi-retired) who value **reliable payouts**, **worthwhile time-to-earnings ratio**, and **transparency** above all else. "Fun" scored literally zero as a priority — this audience wants to be respected, not entertained. Trust is the #1 acquisition driver and the #1 retention risk.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Trust is everything.** "Proof it pays out" is the #1 reason to try a new app (35%). Unreliable payouts are a top churn driver (45%).
2. **The pennies problem is real.** 53% would quit if earnings feel too small. Users want $25–50/month minimum to feel it's worth their time.
3. **Show real dollars, not points.** 51% prefer dollar amounts; only 11% want a points system.
4. **PayPal is non-negotiable.** 80% use it regularly, 69% want payouts via PayPal. Must be the launch payout method.
5. **Speed matters.** 73% expect payouts within 24 hours. 42% want instant.
6. **Cross-promotion is the #1 acquisition channel** (40%), followed by ads + social media (44% combined).
7. **Core activity loop:** Videos (87%), games (79%), daily check-ins (77%) — these three are the engagement foundation.

## Strategic Implications for Product & Brand

### Product Priorities
1. **PayPal payouts at launch** — 69% preferred, 80% already use PayPal
2. **Fast payout processing** — Target instant or same-day; 73% expect <24 hours
3. **Low cashout minimums** — High minimums are a top-3 churn driver (45%)
4. **Core loop: videos + games + daily check-ins** — All score 77%+ interest
5. **Show earnings in real dollars** — Not points, not coins, not tokens

### Brand & Tone Calibration
- **"Fun to use" scored 0%.** Playfulness should be a seasoning, not a main ingredient.
- Tone should feel **reliable, straightforward, and respectful** — not cute or gamified
- The Savi mascot approach needs to stay grounded; the EarnWise clarity resonates with what users actually value
- This audience responds to **proof and transparency**, not personality and charm

### Acquisition Strategy
- **Cross-promotion from existing apps** is the #1 channel (40%) — leverage MEA ecosystem
- **Social proof is critical** — payout screenshots, testimonials, app store ratings above 4.3
- **Sign-up bonus** as a hook for 20% of potential users
- **Facebook** is the social platform (61% use it regularly) — prioritize for paid social

### Earnings Framing
- Users spend on **everyday expenses (55%)** and **treats (41%)** — both framings are valid
- Goal-based framing ("let's get to $10 this week") may feel more empowering than expense anchoring ("that's half a tank of gas") per team feedback
- Target **$25–50/month** earnings — the sweet spot for 40% of users
- Avoid anything that feels like "pennies" — the #1 churn trigger
