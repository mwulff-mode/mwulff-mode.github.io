Who it's for

A product for the woman the category has overlooked.

EarnWise is a rewarded Android app for US women 45 and up. She's financially cautious, not desperate. She manages a household budget, plays casual games during quiet moments in her day, and deletes anything that feels scammy within thirty seconds. Most apps in this category are built for someone twenty-five years younger than her. We are building this one for her, on purpose, and welcoming everyone else along.

Phase 1 is one railroaded earn loop — sign in, profile, survey, install a game, hit a milestone, cash out to PayPal. Real dollars. No points. No tricks. The brand has to carry the trust before any feature can.

What it looks like

The first five moments.

Five screens that carry the brand most of the way: the cover, the explainer, the home, a task in depth, and the payoff. Everything below is in service of these.

Brand attributes

Three values to pass. Three guardrails never to fail.

Every feature, screen, and sentence has to pass at least one value. If it ever feels like one of the guardrails, something is wrong and the work goes back.

Values

One
Simplicity
One glance tells her what to do and what she'll get.
Two
Reliability
It works. Earnings arrive on time. No surprises.
Three
Valuable
Her time produces real, visible returns. Dollars, not points.

Guardrails — what we are not

Never
Confusing
One clear action per screen. No jargon, no hidden mechanics.
Never
Spammy
No countdowns. No FOMO. We notify only when it matters.
Never
Cold
We sound like a helpful neighbor, not a corporation.
Voice & feel

How it should sound, and how it should land in her hand.

Encouraging, not fluffy. Clear, not cold. Trusted, not trendy.

The pattern in copy is to state what happened, state the value, and move on. "Nice — you just earned $0.25. It adds up faster than you think." Not "Ka-ching! $0.25 earned! Keep going to unlock more!"

It should feel

  • Trustworthy
  • Calm and clear
  • Respectful of her time
  • Practical and real

It should not feel

  • Too-good-to-be-true
  • Hyped or youth-coded
  • Pushy or desperate for attention
  • Gimmicky or flashy
Visual system

Warm paper, navy authority, coral warmth, teal for the moments that matter.

Illustration

Hand-drawn ink linework on cream paper. Real, everyday objects — wallets, envelopes, clipboards, hands clapping. Two accent colors only, never more. Hands are shown as hands, never full figures or faces, which keeps the work inclusive across age and body type. The opposite of stock vector art. The closest reference is editorial spot illustration, not app art.
