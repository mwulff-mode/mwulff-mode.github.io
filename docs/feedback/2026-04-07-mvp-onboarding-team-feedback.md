### Follow-up Comments
- On RV: I think the idea of the streak, bonus, whatever could be worked in here. But I don't know that RV needs to be in the onboarding task.
- Overall — like the progress. There's a lot of details missing here on the functionality, and I do think that's all important. But I know that can be ironed out. Happy to provide a lot more specific feedback if it's helpful at this point.

### On Personalization
- Interesting that the plan would be to change onboarding tasks based on user answers. Potentially open to this and it's a nice UX, but it certainly makes things more complicated technically. Skeptical that we'll have that many tasks in the app that "personalization" makes a lot of sense.
- The plan was to have a gated / railroaded flow in the NUX. Users have to complete the NUX tasks to move forward and to get the welcome gift. Perhaps we can soften it by opening up the gate after 24hrs, but it's otherwise a forced experience. D1 ROAS is the goal here, so we need to be thoughtful about tasks and how it backs out financially.

### On Welcome Gift / Minimum Cashout
- Agree with the welcome gift working if we do minimum cash out amount. BUT — a minimum cashout amount feels a bit scammy / spammy / untrustworthy. At least to me. It's a bit of a bait and switch. Open to testing this, potentially, but I think it might be further down the road rather than a MVP feature. If anything — we need to make sure this works out well financially and from a UX perspective, and that means it probably needs to be scripted — not truly personalized.

### On NUX Tasks
- NUX tasks are probably a combination of games and/or surveys.
- Initial thought: start with 1 or 2 games that a user needs to:
  1. Download and Install
  2. Get to X milestone in each game
- 1 game would work, especially if we have a survey.
- For surveys, a new user needs to fill out their "profile" survey for the given partner before they get to do monetized surveys. That profile survey is usually margin neutral. So any survey probably needs to be:
  1. Fill out your profile
  2. Complete 1 survey

## Key Themes

1. **Welcome gift needs to be earned, not given** — team consensus is tasks should unlock the payout, not a free gift.
2. **Tasks must be specific and backed by real monetization** — profile survey + monetized survey + 1 game with milestone.
3. **Personalization is nice UX but adds complexity** — keep it scripted for MVP, gated NUX flow.
4. **D1 ROAS is the metric** — every onboarding task must have a financial justification.
5. **Social proof needs work** — avatar letters are confusing, dollar amounts need anchoring.
6. **Goal ring should feel achievable** — daily goals over monthly to show faster progress.
7. **Game selection should offer choice** — at least 3 options, not a single recommendation.
