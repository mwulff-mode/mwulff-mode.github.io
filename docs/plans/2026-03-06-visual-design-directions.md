# Visual Design Directions — A/B Test

> **Purpose:** Pick the visual direction for the app. Same product, two different looks. Choose what feels right.
>
> **How to use this:** Look at the mockups, read the one-liners, vote. Tell us what you like and what you don't — we'll use your feedback to refine the final design.

---

## Direction A: "Soft Piggy Bank"

**In one line:** Warm, nurturing, like opening a well-organized kitchen drawer.

**Palette:** Sage green, blush pink, lavender — on a warm cream background.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Sage | #5E8A60 |
| Secondary | Blush | #A36B63 |
| Accent | Lavender | #7568A0 |
| Background | Warm cream | #FAF8F5 |
| Text | Warm ink | #2D2A26 |

**Font:** Nunito — rounded, friendly, approachable.

**Visual traits:**
- Generous rounded corners
- Soft shadows, no hard edges
- Warm and breathing with white space
- Dollar amounts are big and clear

**Feels like:** Headspace meets Monzo.

**Trust signal:** "This feels safe and nice."

---

## Direction B: "Smart & Clean"

**In one line:** Confident, modern-minimal, quietly premium.

**Palette:** Teal, amber, slate — on a cool off-white background.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Teal | #1A6B6A |
| Secondary | Amber | #B8860B |
| Accent | Slate | #3E5170 |
| Background | Cool off-white | #FAFAF9 |
| Text | Near black | #1C1C1B |

**Font:** DM Sans — clean, modern, warm but precise.

**Visual traits:**
- Clean grid, structured layouts
- Moderate corners, intentional not cutesy
- Flat design with very subtle depth
- Dollar amounts presented with quiet confidence

**Feels like:** Acorns meets Linear.

**Trust signal:** "This feels professional and real."

## What We're Asking

1. Which direction feels more like **you**?
2. What do you **like** about each?
3. What would you **change**?
