## Direction A: "Soft Piggy Bank"

```text
Design a polished mobile app home screen for a savings and rewards app. iPhone portrait screen, realistic shipped product quality. The mood is calm, warm, trustworthy, and nurturing.

Background: warm cream (#FAF8F5). Primary color: muted sage green (#5E8A60). Secondary: blush pink (#A36B63). Accent: soft lavender (#7568A0). Text: warm dark brown (#2D2A26). Typography: Nunito or similar rounded sans-serif, large and friendly.

Layout from top to bottom:
1. Status bar showing 9:41
2. Greeting line "Good morning, Lisa" with a small blush-pink avatar circle on the right
3. Large balance card with a very subtle sage-green tint background and soft sage border. Shows "$12.40" in large bold type, "Today +$1.25" in sage green with a small upward trend arrow. Below a thin divider: "Next cashout" label on left, "$12.40 of $25.00" on right, with a fully rounded pill-shaped sage-green progress bar filled to about 50%.
4. A wide rounded pill button in sage green reading "Start earning" with a subtle green glow shadow
5. Section header "Available now" with "See all" link in sage green
6. Three task cards with generous rounded corners and soft warm shadows: "Quick poll · 3 min · $0.40" with lavender icon circle, "Receipt scan · 2 min · $0.60" with blush icon circle, "Short survey · 5 min · $0.75" with sage icon circle. Dollar values in sage green on the right.
7. Small centered trust line with shield icon: "Paid out via PayPal"
8. Bottom navigation bar: Home (active, sage green with subtle sage pill highlight), Activity, Rewards, Profile

Generous white space everywhere. Very rounded corners on all elements (16-24px radius). Soft feathered shadows, no hard edges. The overall feel is Headspace meets Monzo — warm, breathing, approachable. No neon, no clutter, no gamified coins, no mascots, no hype copy. Dollar amounts are the hero.
```

## Direction B: "Smart & Clean"

```text
Design a polished mobile app home screen for a savings and rewards app. iPhone portrait screen, realistic shipped product quality. The mood is confident, modern-minimal, and quietly premium.

Background: cool off-white (#FAFAF9). Primary color: deep teal (#1A6B6A). Secondary: warm amber (#B8860B). Accent: slate blue (#3E5170). Text: near black (#1C1C1B). Typography: DM Sans or similar clean modern sans-serif, precise and warm.

Layout from top to bottom:
1. Status bar showing 9:41
2. Greeting line "Good morning, Lisa" with a small slate-blue avatar circle on the right
3. Large balance card in clean white with a 3px teal top border accent and minimal shadow. Shows "$12.40" in large bold type, "Today +$1.25" in teal with a small upward trend arrow. Below a thin divider: "Next cashout" label on left, "$12.40 of $25.00" on right, with a slightly rounded teal progress bar filled to about 50%.
4. A moderately rounded button in teal reading "Start earning" with no drop shadow, flat and confident
5. Section header "Available now" with "See all" link in teal
6. Three task cards with moderate rounded corners (12px) and 1px gray borders, no shadow: "Quick poll · 3 min · $0.40" with slate-blue icon circle, "Receipt scan · 2 min · $0.60" with amber icon circle, "Short survey · 5 min · $0.75" with light teal icon circle. Dollar values in teal on the right.
7. Small centered trust line with shield icon: "Paid out via PayPal"
8. Bottom navigation bar: Home (active, teal colored with small teal dot indicator below), Activity, Rewards, Profile

Clean structured layout. Moderate corner radius on elements (8-16px). Very subtle shadows or none — borders define structure instead. The overall feel is Acorns meets Linear — professional, clear, trustworthy. No neon, no clutter, no gamified coins, no mascots, no hype copy. Dollar amounts are the hero.
```
