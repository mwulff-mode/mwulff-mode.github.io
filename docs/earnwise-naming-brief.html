Naming brief

What this app is, who it's for, and the shape of a name that fits.
Working name · EarnWise Phase 1 · June 2026
Product

A rewarded app that pays in real dollars.

Small, reliable rewards for things she already does on her phone, paid out in real money — no points, no tokens. Built to be trusted in thirty seconds and useful in five minutes.

Core value proposition

Her time, turned into real dollars. Calm, transparent, no tricks.

Main earning methods

  • Surveys — quick opinion tasks, paid per completion.
  • Games — install a game, hit a milestone, get paid.
  • Cashout — direct to PayPal. The proof the system works.
Audience

Women 45+, US. Cautious, not desperate.

  • Financially cautious.
  • Scam-aware. Deletes anything that feels off.
  • Wants predictability and visible trust.
  • Responds to a calm, respectful tone.
  • Avoids hype, casino energy, and anything that looks gimmicky.
Emotional goal

What she should think when she reads the name.

"This feels safe."
"This feels practical."
"I understand this immediately."
"This won't waste my time."
Naming constraints

The shape of a name that fits — and the shapes that don't.

Avoid
  • Crypto or startup energy.
  • "Hustle / grind / max / boost" language.
  • Childish, gamified, or cartoonish feel.
  • Hard-to-spell or invented SaaS words.
  • App-store-sensitive terms (cash, earn, money).
Prefer
  • Calm and trustworthy.
  • Understandable on first read.
  • Real English words over coinages.
  • Warm but grown-up.
  • Pronounceable, spellable, searchable.
Directional examples

Not for use — for orientation.

Right neighborhood
Tally Gather Worthy Earnest Flourish
Wrong neighborhood
CashBlaze Grindify RewardStorm CryptoMint BoostUp

The right name should sound like something she'd recommend to a friend over coffee — not something she'd have to defend.
